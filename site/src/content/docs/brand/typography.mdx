## Font files

The web fonts live in [`fonts/`](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Design/tree/main/fonts) and
are wired up by `tokens/tokens.css`:

| File | Face |
|---|---|
| `Gilroy-Regular.woff`, `-Medium`, `-SemiBold`, `-Bold` | Gilroy 400, 500, 600, 700 |
| `Unbounded-Variable.woff2` | Unbounded 500–600 (SIL Open Font License) |

:::caution[Outfit is legacy]
Gilroy replaced Outfit as the body face on 15 October 2026. `Outfit-Variable.woff2`, `Gilroy-Light` and
`Gilroy-ExtraBold` stay in `fonts/` only for older consumers. Don't use them on new surfaces.
:::

Gilroy is not an open-source font. The files are included at the owner's request; use them under the
applicable Gilroy licence. See the [credits](/about/credits/) for details.
