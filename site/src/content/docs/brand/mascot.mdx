## Meet Patch

Patch is an **African painted wolf** (African wild dog) with huge round ears and a patchwork coat. It
carries a wrench in its mouth, like a dog bringing back a stick: borrow it, use it, bring it back.

<MascotGallery />

Every file is in [`mascot/`](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Design/tree/main/mascot), cut
from the two approved concept sheets
([pose and sneeze](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Design/blob/main/mascot/concept/patch-concept-sheet.webp),
[walk](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Design/blob/main/mascot/concept/patch-walk-sheet.webp)).
Nothing was redrawn. Each piece comes three ways, and again for dark surfaces:

| Folder | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `mascot/` | **Full vectors**: traced at the most faithful settings measured, plus large PNGs and WebPs rendered from them, and GIFs of the loops | Big screens, print, merch, anything scaled up |
| `mascot/original/` | **The original pixels**, cut from the sheet exactly and saved losslessly | Editing, retouching, new exports: nothing is lost |
| `mascot/compressed/` | **Compressed copies**: a lighter SVG, the full SVG gzipped as `.svgz`, and a small animated WebP | Product UI and anywhere size matters |
| `mascot/dark/` | **Patch for dark surfaces**: every piece with a thin Warm Cream sticker border, as full SVGs, lossless PNGs and WebPs, and GIFs of the loops | Deep Ink and the dark theme's panels only |
| `mascot/dark/compressed/` | **Compressed dark copies**: lite SVGs, `.svgz` and small WebPs | Product UI on dark surfaces |

| Piece | Files |
|---|---|
| Hero pose | `patch.svg`, `patch.png` |
| Sneeze loop | `patch-animated.svg`, `patch-animated.webp`, `patch-animated.gif`, and eight frames in `frames/` |
| Walking pose | `walk/patch-walk.svg`, `walk/patch-walk.png` |
| Walk cycle | `walk/patch-walk-animated.svg`, `walk/patch-walk-animated.webp`, `walk/patch-walk-animated.gif`, and eight frames in `walk/frames/` |
| Walking left | `walk-left/patch-walk-left.svg`, `walk-left/patch-walk-left-animated.webp`, `walk-left/patch-walk-left-animated.gif`, and eight frames in `walk-left/frames/` |
| On dark surfaces | Every file above under the same name in `dark/`, for example `dark/patch.svg`, `dark/patch-animated.gif` and `dark/walk-left/frames/` |

**Walking left** is the approved way to move Patch to the left. The script mirrors the walk cycle
exactly, so it shows Patch's other side; frame N lines up with walk frame N for a mid-stride turn.

The same names sit in `original/`, `compressed/` and `dark/`. In product UI, use the animated WebPs;
they're far lighter than the animated SVGs. Browsers only show `.svgz` when the server sends it
gzip-encoded, so use the plain `.svg` on web pages. The GIFs are for email and chat tools that can't play
an animated WebP: GIF has only 256 colours and hard-edged transparency, so use the WebP or SVG wherever
they work.

**On dark surfaces**, Patch's thick dark outline almost disappears against Deep Ink, so use the same file
from `dark/`: its thin Warm Cream die-cut border keeps Patch readable, and the art inside is untouched.
Frames and loops keep their canvas, so a dark file drops in for its light twin. On Warm Cream, white and
the light brand colours, use the plain files.

A vector is made of flat colour shapes, so it can't hold soft shading: the subtle brown iris inside each
eye merges into the pupil. That detail is kept in `original/`. To rebuild everything after replacing a
sheet, run `node scripts/cut-mascot.mjs`, then `node scripts/dark-mascot.mjs` and
`node scripts/gif-mascot.mjs`, from `site/`.

## Why a painted wolf

We wanted a mascot whose real behaviour matches what Space Works stands for.

| What painted wolves do | What it says about Space Works |
|---|---|
| **No two coats are alike.** | A community of very different makers, all one pack. |
| **They vote by sneezing.** Once enough of the pack sneezes, it moves ([Walker et al., 2017](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2017/09/something-to-sneeze-about--democratic-voting-in-african-wild-dog)). | The community decides together. Everyone gets a voice. |
| **They look after everyone.** Pups eat first; the pack brings food back for the injured, sick and old ([Tsavo Trust](https://tsavotrust.org/it-takes-a-pack-the-extraordinary-family-life-of-african-wild-dogs/)). | A commons: access for everyone, nobody left behind. |
| **They succeed as a team.** | Collaboration over lone genius. |
| **They are endangered.** | A real story worth telling. |

Sneeze votes aren't equal: about three sneezes do it when the dominant pair joins in, about ten
otherwise. Say "everyone gets a voice", not "one dog, one vote".

The maker world is full of robots and skull-and-wrench marks, and we found no tool library with a
mascot and no software brand using a painted wolf.

## Why "Patch"

The **patchwork coat** that makes every painted wolf unique, **patching things up** in repair and maker
culture, and a **software patch**. Short, friendly and easy to say in any language.

## Colour

The badge and accents use the [brand colours](/brand/colour/): a Maker Teal badge, a Warm Yellow orbit and
planets, and Citron, Workshop Orange and Sky Blue sparkles. The coat's tans and browns and the silver
wrench belong to the artwork; don't reuse them as UI colours.

## Animation

The concept sheet's 7-second loop, frame by frame. Between moments Patch holds the idle frame. The walk
cycle plays its eight frames at 120 ms each.

| Time | Frame |
|---|---|
| 0.0 s | Idle (breathing) |
| 2.0 s | Blink |
| 3.5 s | Left ear twitch |
| 4.0 s | Right ear twitch |
| 5.3 s | Sneeze (prep) |
| 5.6 s | **Sneeze!** The sneeze vote |
| 6.0 s | Recover |
| 6.6–7.0 s | Back to idle |

With reduced motion turned on, the SVG shows the idle frame. In product, save the sneeze (frames 5 to 7)
for moments when the community acts together: a vote passing, a project filling up, a tool coming back to
the shelf.

## Where Patch shows up

Patch is the friendly face. **The box symbol is still the logo.**

| Use Patch for | Use the logo for |
|---|---|
| Empty states, onboarding, loading, success, 404 and error pages | The app header, favicon and app icon |
| The website hero, docs, social posts | Legal pages and formal documents |
| Stickers, merch and event signage | Anywhere the brand must be identified |

Patch counts as the layout's [one bold moment](/brand/graphics/). Don't put Patch inside or beside the box
symbol as a combined mark.

## Keep Patch intact

1. **Don't recolour, filter or redraw** Patch. Use these files as they are.
2. **Don't stretch, skew or mirror** Patch. The coat is asymmetric on purpose. To head left, use the
   `walk-left/` files; never flip the others with CSS.
3. **Don't add effects** such as shadows, glows, gradients or outlines. The one exception is the Warm
   Cream sticker border of the approved `dark/` files; don't draw your own.
4. **Don't make Patch fierce.** No bared teeth, angry brows or hunting poses.
5. **Don't scale up the PNGs or WebPs.** Use the full SVGs when you need bigger.
6. **Keep clear space** of a quarter of the badge height on every side.
7. **Use the `dark/` files on dark surfaces.** Patch's dark ear rims sit close to Deep Ink. Warm Cream,
   white, Citron, Sky Blue and Workshop Orange surfaces all work with the plain files.
