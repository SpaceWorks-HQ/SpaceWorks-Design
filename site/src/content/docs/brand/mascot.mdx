## Meet Patch

Patch is an **African painted wolf** (African wild dog) with huge round ears and a patchwork coat. It
carries a wrench in its mouth, like a dog bringing back a stick: borrow it, use it, bring it back.

<MascotGallery />

Every file is in [`mascot/`](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Design/tree/main/mascot), cut
straight out of the approved
[concept sheet](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Design/blob/main/mascot/concept/patch-concept-sheet.webp)
on transparent backgrounds. Nothing was redrawn.

| File | Use it for | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `patch.png` | The hero pose | 640 × 640 |
| `patch-animated.webp` | The 7-second loop, anywhere an image goes | 272 × 272 |
| `patch-animated.svg` | The same loop as an SVG, timed with CSS; respects reduced motion | 272 × 272 |
| `frames/` | The eight frames, for app animation code or a Lottie | 272 × 272 each |

They're raster at the sheet's own resolution: use the hero up to about 320 px wide and the animation up to
about 136 px. For bigger or print use, export the original artwork larger, replace the sheet and run
`node scripts/cut-mascot.mjs` from `site/`.

## Why a painted wolf

We wanted a mascot whose real behaviour matches what Space Works stands for.

| What painted wolves do | What it says about Space Works |
|---|---|
| **No two coats are alike.** | A community of very different makers, all one pack. |
| **They vote by sneezing.** Once enough of the pack sneezes, it moves ([Walker et al., 2017](https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2017/09/something-to-sneeze-about--democratic-voting-in-african-wild-dog)). | The community decides together. Everyone gets a voice. |
| **They look after everyone.** Pups eat first; the pack brings food back for the injured, sick and old ([Tsavo Trust](https://tsavotrust.org/it-takes-a-pack-the-extraordinary-family-life-of-african-wild-dogs/)). | A commons: access for everyone, nobody left behind. |
| **They succeed as a team.** | Collaboration over lone genius. |
| **They are endangered.** | A real story worth telling. |

Sneeze votes aren't equal: about three sneezes do it when the dominant pair joins in, about ten
otherwise. Say "everyone gets a voice", not "one dog, one vote".

The maker world is full of robots and skull-and-wrench marks, and we found no tool library with a
mascot and no software brand using a painted wolf.

## Why "Patch"

The **patchwork coat** that makes every painted wolf unique, **patching things up** in repair and maker
culture, and a **software patch**. Short, friendly and easy to say in any language.

## Colour

The badge and accents use the [brand colours](/brand/colour/): a Maker Teal badge, a Warm Yellow orbit and
planets, and Citron, Workshop Orange and Sky Blue sparkles. The coat's tans and browns and the silver
wrench belong to the artwork; don't reuse them as UI colours.

## Animation

The concept sheet's 7-second loop, frame by frame. Between moments Patch holds the idle frame.

| Time | Frame |
|---|---|
| 0.0 s | Idle (breathing) |
| 2.0 s | Blink |
| 3.5 s | Left ear twitch |
| 4.0 s | Right ear twitch |
| 5.3 s | Sneeze (prep) |
| 5.6 s | **Sneeze!** The sneeze vote |
| 6.0 s | Recover |
| 6.6–7.0 s | Back to idle |

With reduced motion turned on, the SVG shows the idle frame. In product, save the sneeze (frames 5 to 7)
for moments when the community acts together: a vote passing, a project filling up, a tool coming back to
the shelf.

## Where Patch shows up

Patch is the friendly face. **The box symbol is still the logo.**

| Use Patch for | Use the logo for |
|---|---|
| Empty states, onboarding, loading, success, 404 and error pages | The app header, favicon and app icon |
| The website hero, docs, social posts | Legal pages and formal documents |
| Stickers, merch and event signage | Anywhere the brand must be identified |

Patch counts as the layout's [one bold moment](/brand/graphics/). Don't put Patch inside or beside the box
symbol as a combined mark.

## Keep Patch intact

1. **Don't recolour, filter or redraw** Patch. Use the cut-outs as they are.
2. **Don't stretch, skew or mirror** Patch. The coat is asymmetric on purpose.
3. **Don't add effects** such as shadows, glows, gradients or outlines.
4. **Don't make Patch fierce.** No bared teeth, angry brows or hunting poses.
5. **Don't enlarge the files** past the sizes above. Re-export from the original instead.
6. **Keep clear space** of a quarter of the badge height on every side.
7. **Check dark surfaces.** Patch's dark ear rims sit close to Deep Ink; light surfaces and Workshop
   Orange work best.
