## Sound like a helpful maker

Clear, warm and specific. Make the next step easy.

| Say | Skip |
|---|---|
| <span class="sw-say">Find the right part.</span> | Optimize your asset ecosystem. |
| <span class="sw-say">No parts added yet. Add your first item.</span> | Inventory database is empty. |
| <span class="sw-say">Couldn't save. Try again.</span> | Oops! Something went horribly wrong. |

## Rules of thumb

- **Say what happened, then what to do next.** An empty state names the next action. An error says what
  failed and how to recover.
- **Use the words people use in the space.** Parts, tools, shelves and boxes, not assets and entities.
- **Keep it calm.** No exclamation marks for errors, and no jokes when something went wrong.

## Naming

- In copy, write **Space Works**: two words, both capitalized.
- The wordmark is lowercase **space works** and only ever appears as the logo artwork.
- Keep technical names accurate: the `SpaceWorks` repository, `spaceworks-hq.github.io` and package names
  stay as they are.
