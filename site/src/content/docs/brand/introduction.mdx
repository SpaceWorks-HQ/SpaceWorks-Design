**Less searching. More making.** Space Works is free, open-source software for makerspaces and tool
libraries, and its brand is built the way a good workshop is: open, organized and full of energy.

## The brand at a glance

- **Name.** Write **Space Works** in copy. The wordmark is lowercase **space works**. Keep technical names
  (like the `SpaceWorks` repository) accurate.
- **Logo.** Use the supplied masters. Never stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects. Keep a quarter of the
  symbol height clear on every side. [Logo rules →](/brand/logo/)
- **Colour.** Neutral first, accent second. Seven exact colours, and only tested text pairs. Never orange
  text on cream. Every status gets a word or an icon, not just a colour. [Colour →](/brand/colour/)
- **Type.** Gilroy for everything you read. Unbounded for one short, expressive phrase per layout.
  [Typography →](/brand/typography/)
- **Layout.** Use one bold moment, align to a simple grid, and let empty space do its job.
  [Graphics →](/brand/graphics/)
- **Voice.** Clear, warm and specific. Make the next step easy. [Voice →](/brand/voice/)

## Where it comes from

The visual identity comes only from the
[Space Works brand guidelines](/brand/guidelines/), exported from the Figma file on 15 October 2026. This site
publishes that kit; when the two disagree, the guidelines win.
