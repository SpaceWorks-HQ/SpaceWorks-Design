## Built from the box

Use the flap shapes as quiet accents. Keep the logo itself intact.

1. **Use one bold moment.** One teal panel, one orange flap, one Unbounded phrase. Not all three at once.
2. **Align to a simple grid.**
3. **Let empty space do its job.**

## Shape

Taken from the brand's own product mock:

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Controls (buttons, inputs) | 10 px radius |
| Cards and windows | 16 px radius |
| Tags and pills | Fully round |
| Shadows | None |
| Hairlines | Deep Ink at 18% |

## Icons

Simple icons, real objects, a makerspace people recognize.

- **24 px grid, 2 px stroke.**
- **Rounded ends, consistent weight.**
- **Always pair unfamiliar icons with labels.** Navigation is text-only.

## Imagery

**Show the real space:** parts, hands and shared benches, in soft daylight with honest materials. Keep the
work in focus.

Avoid staged tech clichés and busy backdrops.

## A place for every part

Carry the same identity from the screen to the shelf. On labels and item cards:

- **Name first.** Keep the item name large and easy to scan.
- **Details second.** Use Gilroy for clear IDs and shelf codes.
- Keep labels consistent, and test their size and contrast at the intended viewing distance.
