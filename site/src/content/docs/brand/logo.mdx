## One name. A flexible family.

Use the supplied masters. Keep the spelling and the lowercase wordmark intact. Every master below is the
real file from [`logo/`](https://github.com/SpaceWorks-HQ/SpaceWorks-Design/tree/main/logo), as an SVG
plus a 4× PNG.

## Choose the right background

Preserve every panel, the internal gaps and the wordmark's proportions.

| Surface | Use |
|---|---|
| Warm Cream, white or a light accent | The **full-colour** logo |
| White, single-colour print | The **one-colour ink** master |
| Deep Ink or Maker Teal | The **reversed** cream master |

The monochrome versions keep a hollow opening and transparent folds. Don't make them by recolouring the
full-colour logo.

## Room to breathe

Keep the lockup together and measure the visible artwork. **H** is the symbol height; scale the master as one
object.

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Symbol to stacked wordmark height | **1.25×** |
| Gap between symbol and wordmark | **H ÷ 6** |
| Clear space on every side | **H ÷ 4** |

## Keep it legible

These are recommended starting sizes. Always check the final output.

| Where | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Symbol height, on screen | **48 px** |
| Primary lockup width, on screen | **180 px** |
| In print | **12 mm** symbol / **40 mm** lockup. Proof at actual size before production. |
| App icon | Symbol ≈ **62%** of the tile height, on a warm cream field |

Below 48 px, test a dedicated small-size asset rather than shrinking the master.

## Keep the system intact

The box is a fixed piece of artwork. Treat it as one recognizable unit.

1. **Don't stretch** it.
2. **Don't rotate** it.
3. **Don't recolour** the panels.
4. **Don't lose the dark panels** on a dark background. Use the reversed master instead.
5. **Don't add effects** such as shadows, glows or gradients.
6. **Don't change the type** of the wordmark.
