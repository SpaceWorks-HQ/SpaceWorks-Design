## Seven colours. One clear system.

Exact sRGB values: three core colours, three accents and a warm canvas. These come straight from

## Neutral first, accent second

Most of any layout is Warm Cream and Deep Ink. Teal carries actions, orange is the one moment of energy,
and the pastels stay small.

- **Maker Teal** is the primary brand colour and the action colour.
- **Workshop Orange** is energy and emphasis: attention counters, low-stock tags, one bold moment per layout.
- **Citron** can fill whole sections. **Warm Yellow** is a small accent with Deep Ink text.

## Readable by design

Use only tested text pairings, and add a word or an icon to every status.

<Pairings />

- Normal text needs **≥ 4.5:1**; large text needs **≥ 3:1**. Large means 24 px regular or 18.7 px bold.
- **Avoid orange text on cream.** It only reaches 2.47:1.
- Ratios follow WCAG 2.2 *Contrast (Minimum)* and *Use of Color*.

The full light and dark palettes the app uses, with every ratio computed, are in the
[token reference](/tokens/reference/) and the [UI brand spec](/specs/ui-brand-spec/).
