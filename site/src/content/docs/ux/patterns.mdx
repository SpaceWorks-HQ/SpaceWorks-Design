These are real screens from **Space Works Events**, built on Space Works UX and run with demo data. Each pattern
links back to its number in the [Space Works UX spec](/specs/ux-patterns/). Select any image to open it at full
size.

## App shell and sidebar (P1)

A Citron sidebar with the workspace switcher on top, a text-only nav with one filled active item, then "View
public page ↗" and the account chip at the bottom. On a phone it folds into a top bar and sheet.

<UxShot name="workspace-dashboard" alt="Organisation dashboard in the app shell" phone caption="The dashboard answers “what needs me?” first: the callout links straight to the approvals and the draft." />

## Page header, callout and stats (P2, P4, P5)

One identity block per page: name, meta and at most one filled primary action. A contextual callout carries the
next step, and stat cards show real numbers.

<UxShot name="detail-overview" alt="Event detail with header, stat cards and readiness callout" phone caption="Detail header with a date tile, status chips, underline tabs and a readiness checklist where every gap links to its fix." />

## Lists with filter chips and bulk actions (P6, P8, P10)

Filter chips carry their counts. Selecting rows turns on the bulk bar. Every status is a word in a pill, never
colour alone.

<UxShot name="list-member-ids" alt="Member list with filter chips, search and status pills" phone caption="Member IDs: All 10 · Waiting 2 · Approved 6 · Rejected 1 · Revoked 1, with a reason shown under a rejection." />

<UxShot name="list-guests" alt="Guest list with status chips and inline actions" phone caption="Guests: the next step is inline in the row; rare actions sit in the row menu." />

## Tables inside a card (P14)

Dense data keeps the card chrome: a bordered card, sentence-case column labels and a hairline between rows.

<UxShot name="table-team" alt="Team table with roles and an invite card" caption="Team members with roles, and the invite card on top." />

## Settings rows, live preview and sticky save (P15, P16)

Settings are labelled rows with helper text beside each field. Changes collect in a sticky Discard / Save bar,
and the danger zone sits at the very end.

<UxShot name="settings-organisation" alt="Organisation settings with live public-page preview" phone caption="The public-page preview updates as you type. Nothing is saved until you choose Save changes." />

## Create with a live preview (P18)

Create forms stay short, mark optional fields inline, and check the web address as you type.

<UxShot name="form-new-organisation" alt="Create an organisation form" />

## Empty states and first-run (P17)

An empty workspace never just says "nothing here". It names the next step, and the first-run checklist shows only
one call to action at a time.

<UxShot name="workspace-empty-state" alt="New organisation with the first-run checklist" />

## Work in a focused flow (P21, P30)

High-frequency work gets its own focused surface: the scanner, search, live progress and an offline callout.
Reversible actions offer Undo in place instead of asking "Are you sure?".

<UxShot name="flow-check-in" alt="Check-in desk with scanner and progress" phone />

## Auth split screen and centred task card (P22, P23)

Sign-in is a split screen: the form on one side, one bold brand moment on the other. Single tasks, like resetting
a password, get one centred card.
