Space Works UX is our own, and it started from [Yuyu](https://github.com/twilighty-abhi/yuyu), an event
platform by Abhiram N J. We studied 28 of its screens on four boards (R1–R28 in the
[spec](/specs/ux-patterns/)) for layout, navigation and interaction, then adapted them for Space Works.

## What we kept

- A calm workspace: one shell, text-only navigation and one filled primary action per region.
- Stat cards, contextual callouts and lists that put the next step inline.
- Undo in place for reversible actions, and a danger zone for the rest.
- A split-screen sign-in, centred task cards and public pages with a sticky action card.

## What we changed

- **Visual identity.** Every colour, typeface, logo and icon comes from the
  [Space Works brand guidelines](/brand/guidelines/), not from the boards. Yuyu's near-black pill buttons became
  Maker Teal, and its warm-grey sidebar became a Citron field.
- **Scale.** Dense tables, guided multi-step flows, payments with receipts, kiosks and many roles. See
  "Where the reference boards don't scale" in the [spec](/specs/ux-patterns/).

## The reference boards

These boards are Yuyu's work, shown here for attribution and study. They are not part of the Space Works design
